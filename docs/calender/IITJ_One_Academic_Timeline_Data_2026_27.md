# 6. Semester / Teaching-Day Notes

-   If the Government of India announces any additional Holiday on a
    working day, the buffer days of **18 November 2026 for Semester I**
    and **21 April 2027 for Semester II** will be observed as a working
    day.
-   Lectures on the specified timetable-adjustment days will follow the
    schedule listed in the timetable-adjustment tables.
-   If the requisite number of lectures cannot be scheduled, instructors
    may, in consultation with students in their respective courses,
    decide on a schedule of extra classes.
-   A regular course has engagement of **13 lectures (one lecture of 50
    minutes) per one credit**.
-   There will be **no extra classes on Gazetted Holidays**.
-   Saturdays and Sundays falling within the examination period will be
    used for scheduling examinations.
-   Under extraordinary circumstances, examinations of some courses may
    be scheduled on the days preceding the mid-semester/end-semester
    examinations.
-   All faculty members need to necessarily report to the Institute
    before the date of physical reporting of continuing students, as
    specified in the academic calendar.
