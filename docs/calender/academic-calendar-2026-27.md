### Notes (verbatim from source)

- If the Government of India announces any additional holiday on a working day, the buffer days of **18 November 2026** (Semester I) and **21 April 2027** (Semester II) will be observed as working days.
- Lectures on the days below will follow the timetable of the day mentioned against them (see Part B).
- If the requisite number of lectures cannot be scheduled, the instructor(s) may, in consultation with the students in their respective courses, decide on a schedule of extra classes. A regular course has an engagement of 13 lectures (one lecture of 50 minutes) per credit.
- There will be no extra classes on Gazetted Holidays.
- Saturdays and Sundays falling within the examination period will be used for scheduling examinations. Under extraordinary circumstances, examinations of some courses may be scheduled on the days preceding the mid-semester / end-semester examinations.
- **All faculty members need to necessarily report to the Institute before the date of physical reporting of continuing students, as specified in the academic calendar.

## Extraction notes — inconsistencies and things to confirm

All of these are copied as printed above. Confirm with the Office of Academic Affairs before showing them as final in the app.

1. **Last date of course withdrawal request differs between two tables.** Comprehensive row 52 says **1 Oct 2026 (Thu)** / **2 Mar 2027 (Tue)**; the Fractals table says **12 October 2026 (Mon)** / **15 March 2027 (Mon)**.
2. **Display of minor answer sheets (Summer):** only the Fractals table gives **17 June 2027, Thu**; comprehensive row 54 leaves Summer blank.
3. **Holiday list years:** items 1–9 are dated January–June **2026**, i.e. before AY 2026-27 starts (July 2026). The list appears to be the calendar-year 2026 holiday list. Holidays for January–June 2027 (Semester II) are **not yet declared**.
4. **Summer registration:** the summary table gives one merged cell "03-10 May 2027" for all registration items, while comprehensive row 18 gives physical reporting of old students as **09 May 2027, Sun**.
5. **Row 93, Sem II column, says "30 Jul 2027, Fri"** — that date falls after the Summer Term and looks like reporting for the next academic year. Confirm which term it belongs to.
6. **Row 65 National Unity Day** has a blank (not "NA") Sem II cell.
7. **Row 86 Inter IIT Sports and Tech meet** is printed without a year ("10th December to 22nd December (tentative), Thu-Tue"); it sits in the Sem I column, so 2026 is assumed in the data file.
8. **Typos kept/normalized:** "23th January 2027" (Sem II timetable) → 23 January 2027; "Extravagenza" kept as printed.
9. **Buffer working days:** 18 Nov 2026 and 21 Apr 2027 become working days only if the Government announces an additional holiday on a working day.
10. Weekday names in the source were checked against the dates in the data file (see the JSON `meta.validation` block).
